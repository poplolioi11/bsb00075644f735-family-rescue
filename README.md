# 瘋婦投河（《點石齋畫報》辛二第9圖，1886 年，巴伐利亞邦立圖書館） / Feng fu tou he [A Woman in Distress and Her Rescue]: Transcription Dataset

《點石齋畫報》辛二〈瘋婦投河〉記一位京城婦女行為失常，其十六七歲的兒子見狀請旁人相助。記事記下救援與婦女不願離開的反應，也留下報刊對其痛苦的評論。本文是家庭與旁人介入的報刊個案。館方已有影像及公開機器 OCR。此次按圖重讀補足 OCR 的篇章定位與讀序，並分記原欄、圖字、印款與疑讀。

The Dianshizhai Pictorial recounts an incident in Beijing in which a woman in distress went into a canal and her teenage son asked bystanders to assist. The account describes their attempted rescue, her resistance to leaving the water, and the writer’s assessment of her suffering. The holding library already supplies its scan and machine OCR. This edition records complete article boundaries and source reading order, plus the column divisions, labels, marks, and uncertain readings.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。原紀年依原刊印「辛二」。日本 CiNii 目錄列辛集 1886.4–1887.4。依同地同時的 1886-08-06 八十四號售報告白（庚十二）及 1886-12-11 辛集目錄已見付印，辛二可定位在 1886 年。原刊日未定。　圖說正文、圖字、印與版記分列。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Text, caption, illustration labels, artist marks, seals, and page designations are described separately. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
